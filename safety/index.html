Safety & Community

PartnR helps golfers connect. Follow these guidelines to keep rounds welcoming, respectful and safe.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Before meeting

  • Review profiles, round details and messages.
  • Meet at the course or another public place.
  • Tell someone where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transportation when meeting someone new.

On the course

Respect staff and golfers and follow course rules and weather warnings. Never use the app while driving. GPS yardages are estimates and do not replace awareness of hazards, people or property.

Respect and consent

Harassment, threats, hate, unwanted sexual conduct, stalking, impersonation, scams and sharing private information are prohibited. Ask before sharing another golfer's photo, score or details outside the round.

Blocking and reporting

Block unwanted interaction and report genuine concerns for review. Misusing reporting tools may violate the Terms.

Emergencies

PartnR is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services or course staff first if anyone is in immediate danger.

Privacy reminders

Use care when sharing location, travel and schedule information. Choose the narrowest round visibility that fits your group.
